# Workflow 02 — Design Direction

## Goal

Translate the diagnosis into a coherent, prospect-specific website direction before building.

## Decide explicitly

- core customer proposition above the fold;
- primary CTA and any justified secondary CTA;
- navigation and page hierarchy;
- hero structure and message density;
- trust/proof architecture using verified evidence only;
- service grouping and naming in customer language;
- visual personality appropriate to the actual business;
- typography, spacing, contrast and content density;
- imagery strategy using verified company media when available;
- mobile hierarchy and action placement.

## Decision rules

1. Prefer one clear composition over a collection of unrelated sections.
2. Every major block must serve customer comprehension, trust or action.
3. Do not add generic sections such as statistics, testimonials, logos or badges without verified content and a commercial reason.
4. Use customer language rather than database labels, audit terminology or internal jargon.
5. Do not force the same layout on businesses with materially different buying behavior.
6. Variation belongs in the design direction; do not create new SolidDesign infrastructure for one prospect.

## Output

Provide a compact design direction containing:

- design thesis in 1–2 sentences;
- proposed page/section order;
- hero proposition + CTA strategy;
- trust strategy;
- service/content strategy;
- visual direction;
- mobile-specific decisions;
- what will deliberately be omitted.

The direction should be specific enough to build from, but not a speculative design-system document.
