# Business Model — Phase 1 Hypotheses

## Positioning

SolidDesign is not positioned as an “AI website agency”.

The initial proposition is:

> **Convert existing demand better.**

We look for established businesses that already have demand and reputation but whose website creates avoidable conversion friction.

## Ideal prospect

```text
working business
+
existing online demand
+
good reputation
+
existing website
+
high customer value
+
visible conversion leakage
+
standardizable delivery
```

## Acquisition thesis

Proof before purchase:

1. identify a high-opportunity business;
2. audit its current digital conversion path;
3. create a limited personalized concept;
4. send a personalized physical envelope;
5. include before/after visual, three findings and QR/URL;
6. prospect decides whether to engage;
7. human handles sales from real interest onward.

No “no cure no pay” claim in Phase 1.

## Main product hypothesis

### Conversion Rebuild — target test price: €2,950 ex. VAT

Included hypothesis:

- up to 5 core pages;
- mobile-first redesign;
- improved CTA/contact flow;
- technical baseline;
- local SEO basics;
- analytics setup;
- one standard contact form;
- agreed existing-content migration;
- up to 2 correction rounds;
- basic domain/DNS launch support.

Excluded:

- e-commerce;
- portals/member areas;
- complex booking engines;
- custom CRM/ERP integrations;
- full brand identity project;
- photography/video production;
- unlimited copywriting;
- legal-text drafting;
- business e-mail migration;
- unlimited changes;
- ongoing SEO/content campaigns.

Out-of-scope prospects are disqualified or quoted separately later. The MVP product is not broadened to win every lead.

## Recurring hypothesis

### Website Growth Care — target test price: €249/month ex. VAT

Included hypothesis:

- hosting;
- technical maintenance;
- uptime/performance monitoring;
- analytics availability;
- one small content change/month, max 30 minutes;
- quarterly conversion check;
- short quarterly report.

Everything outside this boundary is separately quoted.

## Unit economics

```text
Customer Gross Value
=
Project Revenue
- Delivery Cost
- Acquisition Cost
- Sales Cost
+ Expected Recurring Gross Profit
```

```text
Expected Prospect Value
=
P(win) × Customer Gross Value - Cost To Pursue
```

## Core metric

> Gross profit per 100 qualified prospects.

## Important experiment

A live personalized demo may not be required for every lead.

Test later:

- concept screenshot + concise audit;
- same pack + live interactive demo.

If the live demo adds little response relative to cost, reserve it for the highest-value tier.
